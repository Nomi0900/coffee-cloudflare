Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use this website, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, delivery address (for delivery orders), the items you order and any note you add.
  • Payment reference: if you pay online by transfer, the transaction ID you enter. We do not collect or store card numbers.
  • Usage data: pages viewed and actions such as adding an item to your order, collected through cookies and tracking tools such as the Meta Pixel.

How we use it

  • To prepare, deliver and confirm your order, and to contact you about it.
  • To check that an online payment has been received.
  • To understand how the website is used and to measure and improve our advertising.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the people and services needed to run the website and your order:

  • Our staff and delivery riders, to fulfil your order.
  • Service providers that host the website and store order data (such as Cloudflare).
  • Messaging and email services that notify us of new orders.
  • Meta, for advertising measurement through the Meta Pixel.

Cookies and tracking

The website may use the Meta Pixel to measure the results of our ads. You can limit this through your browser settings, by blocking cookies, or through your Meta ad preferences.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as we need them for our business, accounting and legal obligations, and then delete or anonymise them.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you. Contact us using the details below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We use reasonable measures to protect your information. No online service can guarantee complete security.

Children

This website is not intended for children, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
