Put your product photos here (JPG or WebP, square, about 1000 x 1000 pixels, under 300 KB).
Then in the admin panel, Menu > Edit item, type the photo link like this:   /images/flat-white.jpg
Photos in this folder are static files: free, fast and they never count against any limit.
Also put your WhatsApp share image here (1200 x 630), for example /images/share.jpg, and set ogImage in public/brand.js.
